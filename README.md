# Persona AI Female Voice Generator 🎙️

A local, lightweight, studio-quality AI Female Voice Generator web application.

- **Ultra-Realistic Voices**: Powered by Neural TTS (natural human breathing, intonation, and tone — not robotic).
- **Zero API Keys & 100% Free**: No subscriptions or limits.
- **Phone & PC Support**: Use directly from your computer or open it on your mobile phone on the same Wi-Fi.
- **Instant MP3 Download**: Save your audio recordings with 1 click.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

### Method 1: One-Click (Easiest)
Simply double-click:
```
start.bat
```
This automatically starts the server and opens the app in your browser at `http://localhost:5050`.

---

## 📱 How to Use on Your Phone

1. Connect your phone to the **same Wi-Fi network** as your PC.
2. In the web app, click **"Open on Phone"** in the top-right corner.
3. **Scan the QR Code** with your phone's camera, or type the shown URL into your phone's browser:
   ```
   http://<YOUR-PC-IP>:5050
   ```
4. You can now type scripts and generate voices directly from your phone!
